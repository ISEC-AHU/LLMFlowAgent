This process begins when a potential customer submits an inquiry about a product or service. Upon receiving the inquiry, sales staff or customer support performs two activities in parallel: collecting relevant customer information and addressing the customer’s concerns or questions. Once both activities are completed, the customer is guided through selecting the appropriate product or service. Next, the sales representative provides a quote. After the quote is provided, the customer places the order. The order is then recorded in the system, after which an order confirmation is sent to the customer. Finally, the process ends when the order has been successfully recorded and confirmed.
