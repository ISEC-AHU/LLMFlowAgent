## Evaluation Rules

For each evaluation dimension:

1. Give a score from 1 to 5.
2. Give a specific reason of 35 to 90 words.
3. Use Tool Validation and Log Conformance Results only as internal evidence for determining the score.
4. The reason must:

   * identify the relevant nodes or dependency links;
   * quote or paraphrase the corresponding requirement from user_request;
   * explain whether the DAG satisfies or violates that requirement.
   * when it violates the requirement, explicitly name the missing or extra dependency and explain the resulting workflow risk.
5. Do not mention logs, log support, confidence values, tool validation, type validation, Dependency Gap Analysis, or scoring rules in the reasoning.
6. Pay special attention to ordering expressions such as after, next, once both activities are completed, and in parallel.
7. Treat the authoritative Dependency Links list as the complete current DAG. Never claim that a listed edge is missing and never invent an unlisted edge.
8. A workflow dependency may be a control-flow prerequisite rather than a direct data-transfer edge. Do not reject a user-required ordering solely because one tool's declared output type differs from the next tool's declared input type.
9. Include at least one `evidence_checks` item. Record every DAG fact used in the reasoning as a check. Use only `node_exists`, `direct_edge_exists`, or `path_exists`, and set `observed` to the value you actually observe in the authoritative DAG.

## Scoring Reference
Determine the score based on:
* Whether the evaluated requirement succeeds or fails
* Whether the identified issue affects a minor detail or a critical business outcome
* **5**: Requirement fully succeeds with no meaningful business impact.
* **4**: Requirement succeeds; only minor issues exist.
* **3**: Requirement partially succeeds; the issue has moderate business impact.
* **2**: Requirement largely fails; the issue significantly affects a critical business outcome.
* **1**: Requirement completely fails and prevents the critical business outcome.
A missing dependency does not automatically receive a score of 1; score it according to requirement success and business impact.
