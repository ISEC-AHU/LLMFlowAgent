Your task is to generate evaluation rubrics that assess the quality of task planning and DAG construction.

1. **Structural Integrity & Logical Consistency** - Is the DAG structurally complete and acyclic? Are dependencies logically sound? Is the overall data flow coherent without contradictions?

3. **Modality & Type Consistency** - Do input/output modalities and types match correctly between connected tools? Are necessary data format conversions handled properly?
4. **Tool Semantic Matching** - Are the selected tools semantically appropriate for each step? Do the tools' capabilities align with the intended operations required by the task?

- A clear **Theme** (dimension name)
- 3-5 specific **Tips** (actionable evaluation criteria)
- Brief explanation of why this matters
